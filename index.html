Foundational questions

Questions our research tries to answer.

How does infrastructure reorganise urban and regional space?

High-speed rail, metro systems, airports and logistics as catalysts of spatial and economic restructuring.

How can urban systems become observable and modelable?

Digital twins and data pipelines integrating mobility, assets, spatial information and user feedback.

How can spatial intelligence improve access and inclusion?

Routing, accessibility, street-level observation and behavioural evidence for more inclusive urban environments.

How can computation strengthen planning and design judgement?

AI, parametric methods and simulation used as decision-support rather than as substitutes for planning reasoning.
